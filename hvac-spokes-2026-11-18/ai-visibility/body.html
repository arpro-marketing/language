What is AI visibility for an HVAC company?

AI visibility is whether your company gets named when a homeowner asks an AI assistant for a recommendation, and what the assistant says about you when it does. It’s one part of our HVAC marketing agency work, and we judge it the same way as everything else we run: by whether it ends in sold jobs, not by how often your name appears.

It overlaps with local SEO, but it isn’t the same thing. Google’s map pack ranks companies by distance, relevance and prominence. ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google’s AI answers read your reviews, the lists and articles that mention you, and your own website, and then write a short answer. In our research they often land on different companies than the map does.

What one AI recommendation is worth to an HVAC company

A homeowner whose air conditioning quits in July isn’t looking for a list of twenty contractors. When they ask an AI assistant, they get three to five names and a sentence about each. If your company is one of them, you’re in a very short conversation. If it isn’t, that homeowner never calls.

The value depends on which calls those are. A repair visit averages around $350, and a full system replacement around $7,500 (Angi, 2026). One extra replacement a month from AI answers is about $90,000 a year in revenue at that average, before margin. That’s an illustration of the math, not a client result, and it only holds if the call gets answered, the visit gets booked and the estimate gets followed up.

AI visibility for HVAC companies: common questions

If my HVAC company ranks in the map pack, will AI recommend it too?

Often for the top spot, but not reliably for the rest. In our HVAC data, the company AI named first was usually one Google already showed. Across all the HVAC companies AI named, though, about six in ten were outside Google’s 3-pack and top 10, so a map ranking doesn’t guarantee you’re in the answer.

Do schema markup or an llms.txt file get an HVAC company into AI answers?

We haven’t seen evidence that they do. In our study, schema markup made no measurable difference to which businesses AI named, and we haven’t tested llms.txt. Reviews, recent reviews and mentions on other websites were the signals that lined up with being named.

Can national franchises crowd a local HVAC company out of AI answers?

In the local answers we tested, they mostly didn’t. When we asked for the best HVAC company in a city, nearly every company named was a locally branded company, and national franchise names made up only 3 to 5% of mentions. National studies that mix in brand and software questions show franchises doing better, so it depends on what the homeowner asks.

How do I find out whether AI recommends my HVAC company?

Ask the way a homeowner would: “best HVAC company in [your city]” and “who should I call for a new AC in [your city],” in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google’s AI Mode, and repeat each one a few times, because answers change from run to run. Note who gets named and which websites the answer cites. That list tells you where the evidence about your competitors lives.

How long does it take to start showing up in AI answers?

We don’t promise a timeline. The answers change from month to month and from engine to engine, which is why we re-check every month and report what moved, alongside the calls and jobs that came from it.
