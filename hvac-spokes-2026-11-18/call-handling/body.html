What call handling and estimate follow-up mean for an HVAC company

Call handling is everything between the phone ringing and a technician or comfort advisor arriving at the house: answering, booking, confirming. Estimate follow-up is everything between the replacement quote and the homeowner’s decision. Together they make up what we call the second funnel, and they’re part of our marketing for HVAC companies because marketing that stops at the call leaves most of the result to chance.

We work alongside your CSRs, dispatchers and comfort advisors. We don’t replace your team or your call center. We help them see where the jobs are slipping and give them a simple, repeatable way to keep more of them.

How we work with your CSRs and comfort advisors

  1. We listen first. We review a sample of your recorded calls and your call tracking to measure the booking rate for each CSR and each kind of call. The gap between your best CSR and your average one is usually the clearest place to start.
  2. We plan for the busy days. Heat waves and first freezes are when the phones ring most and calls get missed most. We set up overflow and after-hours coverage, a text back to every missed call, and a callback target measured in minutes.
  3. We make the booking ask simple. CSRs offer two specific times instead of asking when works, and a price question turns into a booked visit with a clear diagnostic or consultation fee. Short scripts your team can use on a busy day, not a binder they’ll never open.
  4. We make the replacement easier to say yes to. Clear options at the comfort visit, financing presented as part of the conversation rather than as an afterthought, and a written estimate that leaves the house the same day.
  5. We follow up while the decision is live. A short, focused push in the first ten days after the estimate, while the homeowner is still deciding, then light, automated contact after that. Every open estimate gets reviewed each week. Our estimate follow-up page explains why longer chases cost more than they recover.

HVAC call handling and follow-up: common questions

What is a good booking rate for HVAC CSRs?

Published benchmarks vary too widely to be much use, so we start with your own numbers. Measure the booking rate for each CSR on calls that could have been booked, then compare your average to your best person. That gap, multiplied by your call volume, is what better call handling is worth to you.

How long should an HVAC company follow up on a replacement estimate?

Hard for about ten days, then lightly. Most homeowners who are going to buy decide within a short window after the visit. A focused push of calls and texts in that window wins more jobs than months of reminders, and it costs your team far less time.

Do you replace our CSRs or our call center?

No. We work with the people you have. We measure, coach and set up the systems around them, like overflow coverage, missed-call text-backs and estimate follow-up, so your team books and sells more of the calls you’re already paying for.

Why would a marketing company work on call handling?

Because that’s where the result of the marketing is decided. If half the calls never become a visit, doubling your ad spend mostly doubles the waste. Fixing the second funnel first means every dollar of marketing after that works harder.
