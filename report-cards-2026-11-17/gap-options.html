Report-says cards on phones: three options

Same copy in every option. Each column shows the three homepage cards at real phone width (390px). On desktop these cards stay as they are.

Now (live)

Orange outline, label stack, orange line before the fix.

The report says
“We’re not getting enough leads.”
So you
Increase ad spend.
What’s really happening
New leads wait hours for a callback. They’re booking with competitors.
The fix
Help your team respond in minutes before spending more.
The report says
“Our campaigns are performing well.”
So you
Raise the budget.
What’s really happening
Estimates go out, but too few become sold jobs.
The fix
Improve estimate follow‑up before buying more leads.
The report says
“Let’s push harder before the season.”
So you
Buy more demand.
What’s really happening
Your crews are already booked out for weeks.
The fix
Shift your budget toward higher‑value jobs until capacity opens up.
