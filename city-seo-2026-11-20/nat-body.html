The short answer

What is SEO for contractors?

SEO for contractors is the work that gets a contracting business found when homeowners search for the service it sells: in the Google map pack, in the regular results and in AI answers. It covers your Google Business Profile, your website, your reviews and the listings that confirm who you are and where you work. Rankings are how it starts. We measure it by the calls, booked estimates and sold jobs it produces.

Where the jobs are decided

Three things decide whether SEO books jobs.

The searches that book work.

Some searches bring price shoppers and some bring signed contracts. We map which searches in your market lead to jobs at your price and aim the work there.

The map pack.

For most trades, the three businesses in Google’s map pack take most of the calls. Your Google Business Profile, your reviews and your listings help decide whether you are one of them, along with how close you are to the person searching.

What happens after the call.

A ranking only pays if the call gets answered and the estimate gets followed up. We trace each lead to the booked job, so you can see where opportunities are lost and fix them with your team.

Straight answers

Contractor SEO questions, answered

How much does SEO for contractors cost?
Growth engagements start at $1,500 a month, which covers your website and local search. The full growth engine, with complete SEO and conversion work, starts at $3,500. What fits depends on your trade, your market and how far you are from the top 3. We scope it on a 20-minute call using your own numbers: your job value, your close rate and what a booked job is worth to you. Our tiers are public on the pricing page.
How long does contractor SEO take to work?
Map pack movement often shows within 60 to 90 days. Meaningful organic movement usually takes three to six months, depending on your trade and how contested your market is. For the full method, see the local contractor SEO blueprint.
Should I start with SEO or Google Ads?
Ads can bring leads this week. SEO lowers what each lead costs over time because it compounds. Most growing contractors run both, with ads covering the searches they don’t rank for yet. We’ll tell you which one your numbers say to fund first. See PPC for contractors.
Do you work with contractors outside Tennessee?
Yes. We’re based in Johnson City, Tennessee, and work with contractors nationwide. We take one company per trade in each market.
Do you already work with my competitor?
If we already work with your trade in your market, we tell you on the first call and don’t take the work.
Will my business show up in AI answers too?
AI search uses some of the same signals as Google, but the businesses AI recommends aren’t always the ones Google ranks. We measure where your company appears in ChatGPT, Google’s AI and Perplexity, find why competitors are recommended instead, and work to close those gaps. See AI visibility.
Do you build websites too, or just do SEO?
Both, because the site is half the outcome. When your site is the weak link, we build one around the searches that book work. When it’s solid, we improve what’s there instead.
What’s the commitment?
Terms depend on the size and length of the engagement. Smaller engagements start with a 3- to 6-month commitment, then run month to month with 30 days’ notice. 12-month build-heavy engagements carry a 90-day exit. Whatever the term, your website, your Google Business Profile and your data stay yours.
