What remodeling contractor marketing is

Remodeling contractor marketing, also called home remodeling marketing, is the system a remodeler uses to be found, chosen and booked by homeowners planning a kitchen, bath, addition or whole-home project. Built properly it covers search, ads, reviews, the in-home consult and the follow-up after it, and it is measured to the booked project, not to the lead or the click.

Questions remodelers ask

Remodeling marketing questions owners ask

How do I choose a remodeling marketing agency?
Ask four things. Who owns the website and ad accounts if you leave (it should be you). How an inquiry is counted (a consultation request, not a click). Whether they can show you remodeling project-type pages that rank, not just a homepage. And how results are reported: ask for inquiries tied to booked projects. Whether it calls itself a remodeling marketing agency or a remodeler marketing company, an agency that will not put its price in writing is making a choice; ours is on the pricing page.
What is the difference between remodeling lead generation and remodeling marketing?
Lead generation is the part that creates demand: ranking, ads, exclusive inquiries, response speed. Remodeling marketing is the whole system that turns that demand into booked projects: it also covers the in-home consult, reviews and referrals, crew capacity, and measuring every dollar to the booked job. More inquiries into a system that cannot convert or build them only creates a backlog.
How much should a remodeling company spend on marketing?
It depends on your close rate, your average project value and your stage. Under $1M, buy one predictable channel and prove the cost per booked project. From $1M to $3M, compound what works and add a second channel. At $3M and above, build several channels that reinforce each other. A percentage quoted before anyone asks your close rate is a guess.
What does a remodeling lead cost?
Search-ad inquiries for remodeling run about $150 to $400 depending on market and project type, with cost per click of roughly $8 to $35 for kitchen and bath terms depending on market, and higher for additions and whole-home work (agency benchmarks, BuiltRight Digital and Valley Marketing Group, 2026). Cost per lead is the wrong number to optimize. Cost per booked project, cost per inquiry divided by your close rate, decides whether remodeling marketing pays.
Why do shared leads from HomeAdvisor or Angi close so poorly for remodelers?
Because the same inquiry is sold to three or four contractors at once. The homeowner gets four calls and books with whoever reached them first. Benchmarks published by lead vendors and agencies put shared inquiries at roughly 10 to 20% close against 25 to 40% for exclusive ones you generate yourself. On a five-figure remodel that gap is the difference between a channel that pays and one that drains.
Google Ads or Local Services Ads for a remodeler?
Both, for different jobs. Local Services Ads cost per inquiry and favor remodelers with strong reviews and fast response; they work for bath and kitchen inquiries ready to book. Google Ads reach homeowners earlier in the research, including additions and whole-home work LSAs rarely show. We start with the one your reviews and calendar can support, then add the other.
How long until remodeling marketing produces booked projects?
Paid search can produce consults in the first weeks. Search rankings and reviews compound over three to six months. Response speed and follow-up improve the close rate on the inquiries you already have in the first month, which is usually where we start.
Who owns the website and ad accounts?
You do, from day one. Website, Google Business Profile, ad accounts and all tracking data stay in your name. Ad spend is paid to Google directly from your accounts, never marked up. If we part ways, everything keeps running without us.
Do you work with more than one remodeler in a market?
No. As a remodeler marketing agency we take one remodeling company per market, defined in writing at signing, so you are never competing with our other clients for the same homeowners.
