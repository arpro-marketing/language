Before you pick a service

Questions contractors ask us first

Which service do I actually need?
Usually one, to start — the one fixing your biggest leak. If you’re invisible in search, that’s SEO or GEO. If leads come in but don’t convert, it’s CRM and follow-up. If you can’t tell what’s working, it’s attribution. Not sure? The 6-Gap Revenue Map names it in 60 seconds.
Do I have to buy all of it?
No — and we’ll talk you out of it if you try. We find the single constraint capping your growth, fix that first, and add services only when the numbers say the next one will pay. Growth engineered to reduce risk, not a bundle you don’t need yet.
Do you only work with businesses in Johnson City?
No. We’re based in Johnson City and know the Tri-Cities and East Tennessee market deeply, but there’s no geographic limit — we run these systems for local service businesses, contractors, and national brands across the country. The system travels; the localization is part of the build.
How are your services different from other agencies?
Three ways. We measure to the booked job, not clicks or even leads. We look at both sides of growth — winning the job and having the capacity to deliver it — so we’ll tell you when marketing isn’t your problem. And your leads are exclusive, one partner per market. You own the system and the data when we’re done.
Do you replace the tools and website I already have?
Rarely. We keep what works and connect to it — your CRM, your field software, your site — and rebuild only what’s actually holding you back. The goal is capability inside your business, not a dependency you can never leave.
What does it cost?
It depends on which services you need and how much we’re building versus tuning. It’s structured as a growth engagement, not a per-task invoice, and media is a straight pass-through with no markup. Our tiers are public if you want the ballpark — see pricing — and we’ll scope it precisely before you commit.
