What you won't pay for
  • No setup fees. Work starts on day one at the monthly fee.
  • No ad-spend markup. You pay Google, Meta and the other platforms directly.
  • No shared leads. Every lead belongs to your business alone.
  • No reporting upsells. Attribution, dashboards and competitor intel are included at every tier.
  • Clear exit terms. Smaller engagements: a 3–6 month commitment, then month to month with 30 days' notice. 12-month build-heavy work: a 90-day exit.
  • No exit fees or clawbacks. The work earns its renewal every month.
  • No "lite" tier. Catalyst work meets the same standard as Partnership work. Tiers differ by scope, not quality.

The pricing is the pricing. Nothing on the other side of the contract changes it.
