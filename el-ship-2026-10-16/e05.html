Why generic marketing fails this trade

Marketing for electrical contractors has to answer four numbers.

An agency that runs the same “more calls” plan for every electrician is pricing your generator install like a breaker reset.

1 · The mix
$351 to $5,157

Four trades under one roof

The average electrical job is $351. A panel upgrade is about $1,600 and a standby generator averages $5,157. Every call costs about the same to win. What each one is worth depends on which job it is.

Angi 2026
2 · The clock
Hours, or weeks

Two buyers, two timelines

Someone with a dead circuit calls whoever answers first. Someone pricing a generator compares three companies over weeks. One marketing plan can’t win both.

Buying-cycle pattern; no single benchmark
3 · The crew
81,000 a year

Openings nobody can fill fast

Electrician jobs are projected to grow 9% from 2024 to 2034, with about 81,000 openings a year. For most electrical companies the crew, not demand, is the limit.

BLS Occupational Outlook
4 · The gap
43% booked

Booked isn’t sold

About 43% of electrical Local Services Ads leads book a job. That’s where most reporting stops. For install work, the estimate, the sale and the install all still have to happen.

An electrician marketing system counts what these four add up to, sold and installed work by job type, and builds an engine you keep. Own the pipeline.
