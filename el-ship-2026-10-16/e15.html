What to spend, by stage

How much should an electrical company spend on marketing?

There’s no universal percentage. Anyone quoting “7–10% of revenue” before asking your job mix and close rate is guessing with your money. Pick your stage.

Under $1M

Buy your first predictable channel

One channel that reliably books the work you want, on a site that converts and a claimed Google Business Profile. Prove what a sold job costs, then stop guessing.

$1M–$3M

Shift the mix

Your service calls pay the bills. Now aim new spend at panels, generators and rewires, and track each separately, so you know which is worth more.

$3M–$10M

Build the machine

Search, Local Services Ads, reviews and follow-up running together, measured to the installed job. This is where a second market starts to make sense, if the crews are there.

$10M+

Defend and expand

Protect the markets you own, and open new ones with a model, not a guess.

Timing

Build before storm season, not after it. Generator demand jumps after every major outage, and the companies that rank and have reviews when it hits get the calls. Marketing switched on the week the power goes out is too late.

The question at every stage

What does it cost to win one sold job today, and does the next dollar return more than it costs? Sometimes the crews are the limit and the answer is Not yet.
